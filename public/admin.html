How WhatsApp Checkout Works

Customers never enter payment details on the site. When they hit "Checkout via WhatsApp" or "Buy Now via WhatsApp", the order is logged here as Pending Confirmation, and their WhatsApp app opens with a pre-typed message — item names, quantities, prices, and total — sent straight to your business number. From there you can confirm availability, collect payment however you prefer, and update the order status in the Orders tab as it moves through Confirmed → Shipped → Completed.
